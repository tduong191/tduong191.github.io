Most AI projects start with "build an app." This blog starts from the opposite question: what is the smallest thing I can add to a tool I already use?

## The rule

No new app. No new account. If a spreadsheet, a phone Shortcut, or the Notes app can do the job with a little AI in the gaps, that is the whole build.

## What's coming

- **A gym log in Excel.** One button logs a workout, so I stopped using a separate tracking app.
- **A voice note taker.** I talk, AI summarizes, and the result lands in Notes. It is a Shortcut, the Claude API, and the Notes app. Nothing else.

Each one will be reproducible: a file, a link, or a prompt you can copy and have working the same day. Some will get a part 2, like adding reminders to the voice notes.

## How each post is laid out

1. The problem
2. What I used (existing tools only)
3. How it works
4. How to copy it
5. What broke

The last section is the one I care about most.
